Pluvi:

Pluvi is derived from the Latin word “pluvia”, which literally means rain. It is also the root for the term pluviometer, a device for measuring rainfall.

About Us

Pluvi: is an AI-powered rental station that turns a rainy day into a moment of connection through a simple “take, use, and leave” cycle.

When you scan to unlock an umbrella, you receive a supportive message from a previous stranger like “Today will be okay” to brighten your day.

It’s a seamless sharing system where the umbrella isn’t yours to keep, but yours for now, allowing you to stay dry while passing on a silent thread of kindness to the next person.
